# How to play

## Controls

Guide the fireflies to the score pad in the middle of the map to score points.

Attract fireflies by shining a flashlight of the same color at them.

Steer your circle with touchpad.

Use buttons to shine flash light:

* **N** Red
* **E** Purple
* **S** Green
* **W** Blue

## Points

Fireflies of different color reward different amount of points:

* **Red**     1
* **Purple**  2
* **Green**   3
* **Blue**    5

So, the blue are the best ones!
